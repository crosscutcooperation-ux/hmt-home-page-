We provides industrial project execution solutions for the automotive and auto-component industry, supporting manufacturers with Greenfield plant setup, Brownfield expansion, factory relocation, machine relocation, production-line shifting and manufacturing facility modernization. Automotive facilities often contain interconnected machining, welding, assembly, inspection, material-handling and automation systems, making equipment movement and installation highly dependent on sequencing and layout accuracy. Our project scope can include dismantling, rigging coordination, transportation, positioning, installation, alignment, utility connection and commissioning of CNC machines, VMCs, HMCs, turning centres, grinding machines, press machines, robotic cells, welding systems, conveyors, assembly equipment and related production assets. For new plants, we can support equipment installation according to approved layouts and project requirements; for Brownfield projects, activities can be coordinated around existing machinery, production constraints and shutdown windows. Whether the requirement is relocation of an individual machine, transfer of a complete production line or expansion of an existing automotive plant, our approach is based on equipment characteristics, access conditions, installation requirements and the client's project schedule.

We supports aerospace and defence manufacturing facilities with precision-focused industrial project execution, machinery relocation, equipment installation, factory expansion and Brownfield modernization services. Aerospace and defence production environments may involve CNC machining centres, precision turning and grinding equipment, inspection and testing systems, assembly equipment, automation and specialized manufacturing machinery. Our scope can include equipment survey and planning, dismantling, controlled movement, positioning, installation, alignment, mechanical and electrical connections, utility integration and commissioning support. Projects can be structured for Greenfield facilities where new equipment is being installed or for existing plants where production equipment must be relocated, reconfigured or integrated with new manufacturing infrastructure. Our execution approach is developed around equipment specifications, site access, lifting and handling requirements, installation sequence, technical documentation and project-specific safety procedures.

Pharmaceutical manufacturing environments can contain process equipment, production machinery, filling and packaging systems, inspection equipment, material-handling equipment and associated utilities. Our project scope can include equipment dismantling, movement, positioning, installation, alignment, utility connection and commissioning support, subject to the technical requirements of the facility and applicable project procedures. Brownfield projects can be planned around existing infrastructure, production schedules and shutdown requirements so that new equipment or relocated machinery can be incorporated into the manufacturing environment in a structured manner. From individual equipment relocation to broader plant transfer and expansion projects, We provides coordinated execution support from planning through installation and commissioning.

We provides industrial project execution support for chemical and petrochemical manufacturing facilities where process equipment, mechanical systems, utilities and plant infrastructure require coordinated planning. Whether a manufacturer is relocating a complete facility, transferring selected machines to another building, expanding production capacity or modernizing an existing plant, our project execution can be structured around machine dimensions, weights, access conditions, layout requirements and production priorities.

We provides heavy industrial project execution solutions for steel, metals, foundry and forging facilities, where machinery can involve significant weight, dimensions and specialized handling requirements. Heavy equipment projects require careful consideration of equipment weight, centre of gravity, lifting points, crane and handling requirements, transportation routes, floor loading, foundation conditions and installation sequence.
